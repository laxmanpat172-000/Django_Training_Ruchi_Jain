About SISTec

SISTec is an engineering institute of the Sagar Group of Institutions. The institute focuses on technical education, industry-oriented learning, practical skills and professional development.

The Sagar Group of Institutions was established in 2007 under the aegis of Shri Agrawal Educational & Welfare Society.
